# A-Share News Research Workflow

## Goal

Turn daily A-share news into a short, structured research brief.

## Workflow

1. Collect relevant news.
2. Remove duplicates.
3. Group news by theme.
4. Rank events by importance.
5. Identify affected sectors and companies.
6. Separate facts from interpretation.
7. Generate at least two competing explanations for major events.
8. Identify supporting and contradicting evidence.
9. Assign confidence.
10. Produce an executive summary.

## Output

### Top 3 Events
For each event include:

- What happened
- Why it matters
- Affected sectors
- Supporting evidence
- Counter evidence
- Confidence

### Thinking Map

Show the causal chain:

Event
↓
Transmission mechanism
↓
Sector impact
↓
Company impact
↓
What to watch next

### Research Lesson

Explain one research method the user can learn from today's analysis.
