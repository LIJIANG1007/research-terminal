# Research First Principles

## Overview

This skill defines the fundamental research methodology used throughout
Research Terminal.

Its purpose is not to answer questions faster.

Its purpose is to produce answers that are more reliable, transparent,
and intellectually honest.

Every downstream research skill should inherit these principles.

---

## When to Use

Use this skill whenever the task involves:

- investment research
- company analysis
- macroeconomic analysis
- scientific reasoning
- decision making
- comparing competing hypotheses
- interpreting news
- evaluating evidence
- uncertain or incomplete information

Do not use this skill for simple factual lookups,
basic calculations,
or straightforward formatting tasks.

---

## Core Principles

Always follow these principles.

### 1. Evidence before conclusion

Never start from a conclusion.

Collect evidence first.

Build conclusions from evidence.

---

### 2. Multiple hypotheses

Generate more than one possible explanation whenever uncertainty exists.

Avoid locking onto the first explanation.

---

### 3. Challenge your own reasoning

Before presenting a conclusion,
actively search for evidence that could invalidate it.

Attempt to disprove yourself.

---

### 4. Separate facts from assumptions

Clearly distinguish

Facts

Assumptions

Interpretations

Predictions

Never mix them together.

---

### 5. Quantify uncertainty

Whenever possible,
express confidence numerically.

Example:

Confidence: 82%

instead of

"I'm quite confident."

---

### 6. Update beliefs

New evidence should update previous conclusions.

Do not defend earlier opinions simply because they were stated first.

---

## Standard Research Workflow

For every non-trivial research task,
follow this sequence.

Step 1

Understand the real research question.

↓

Step 2

Collect available evidence.

↓

Step 3

Identify missing evidence.

↓

Step 4

Generate multiple hypotheses.

↓

Step 5

Evaluate supporting evidence.

↓

Step 6

Evaluate contradicting evidence.

↓

Step 7

Estimate probabilities.

↓

Step 8

Reach the current best conclusion.

↓

Step 9

List remaining uncertainties.

↓

Step 10

Recommend what evidence should be collected next.

---

## Output Requirements

Whenever appropriate,
structure answers using the following format.

### Executive Summary

Short conclusion.

---

### Key Evidence

Most important supporting facts.

---

### Competing Hypotheses

Alternative explanations.

---

### Risks

What could make this conclusion wrong.

---

### Confidence

Estimated probability.

---

### Next Research Steps

Recommend additional research.

---

## Failure Modes

Avoid the following behaviors.

- Overconfidence
- Cherry-picking evidence
- Confirmation bias
- Hidden assumptions
- Treating speculation as fact
- Ignoring contradictory evidence
- Providing certainty where uncertainty exists

---

## Goal

The objective of this skill is not to maximize speed.

The objective is to maximize research quality.
