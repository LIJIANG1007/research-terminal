# Research Score

## Purpose

Research Score is a standardized framework for evaluating the importance and quality of research findings.

The total score ranges from 0 to 100.

---

# Scoring Dimensions

| Dimension | Weight |
|-----------|-------:|
| Market Impact | 20 |
| Earnings Impact | 20 |
| Evidence Quality | 20 |
| Persistence | 15 |
| Historical Similarity | 15 |
| Uncertainty | 10 |

---

# Rating Scale

| Score | Rating |
|-------:|:------|
| 90-100 | S |
| 80-89 | A |
| 70-79 | B |
| 60-69 | C |
| Below 60 | D |

---

# Output Format

Research Score: 86 / 100 (A)

Market Impact: 18 / 20

Earnings Impact: 16 / 20

Evidence Quality: 19 / 20

Persistence: 13 / 15

Historical Similarity: 12 / 15

Uncertainty: 8 / 10

Confidence: 82%

---

# Principles

- Scores should be evidence-based.
- Explain every deduction.
- Never hide uncertainty.
- Prefer transparency over precision.
